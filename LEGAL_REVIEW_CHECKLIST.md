# Samith AI — legal approval checklist

The draft/publish interface preserves version history and review references. It does not establish legal compliance.

Qualified local legal counsel should review and approve:
- Regular License rights, commercial/personal use, redistribution and raw prompt resale.
- Extended License rights, embedding/distribution, sublicensing and exclusivity.
- Copyright, creator warranty, third-party rights and marketplace takedown rules.
- Digital goods delivery, consumer cancellation/refund rights, disputes and termination.
- Payment merchant eligibility, provider contracts, taxes, invoices and creator payouts.
- Privacy notice, cookies, subprocessors, data retention, incident management and user rights.
- AI provider terms, prohibited uses and legally valid disclaimers.
- English and all translated versions, effective dates and amendment communication.

Save the reviewer, exact version, dated approval and evidence location. Do not auto-publish templates, invent reviewer names or present AI-generated terms as final legal advice.
